# Privacy policy: List Dialer for Pipedrive

Last updated: 17 September 2026. Published by Serviceform Oy, Finland (https://www.serviceform.com).

## What the extension is
A Chrome extension that helps a salesperson call through a list of contacts in their own
Pipedrive account and log the calls there. It is not affiliated with Pipedrive.

## What data it handles
- **Your Pipedrive API token.** You paste it into the settings. It is stored in Chrome's
  extension storage on your computer only, and sent only to api.pipedrive.com with your requests.
- **Pipedrive records you open.** Names, phone numbers, emails, companies, notes and activities
  of the contacts in the list you are calling. They are read from your Pipedrive account,
  shown to you on screen, and not stored by the extension.
- **What you write.** Your notes, call outcomes and meetings are sent to your Pipedrive account
  and stored there, under your user.
- **Website domains.** To show a screenshot of a company's website, the extension sends that
  website's address to thum.io (https://www.thum.io), a screenshot service, under thum.io's
  own privacy policy. No names, phone numbers or other personal data are sent there.
- **Ringover (optional).** If you enter a Ringover API key, "Call with Ringover" sends the
  prospect's phone number and your own Ringover number to Ringover's API
  (https://www.ringover.com) so Ringover can place the call. The key is stored on your
  computer only.
- **Settings.** Dial mode, countdown length and similar choices, stored on your computer.

## What it does not do
- It does not send any data to Serviceform or to any server of its own.
- It does not collect analytics, and it does not track you.
- It does not sell or share data with anyone.
- It does not record calls. Calls are placed by your phone or your calling app, not by the
  extension.

## Where data goes
| Recipient | Data | Why |
|---|---|---|
| api.pipedrive.com | Your token, the records you view, what you log | It is your CRM |
| image.thum.io | Company website addresses | Screenshots |
| public-api.ringover.com | The number to call and your Ringover number, only when you use the Ringover button | Placing the call |

## Your control
Remove the extension from Chrome to delete its stored settings and token. Data in Pipedrive is
governed by your Pipedrive account and Pipedrive's own privacy policy.
