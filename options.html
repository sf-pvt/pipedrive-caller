List Dialer for Pipedrive

Use your own Pipedrive API token. In Pipedrive: your picture (top right) > Personal preferences > API > copy the token. It only has your own rights, and calls are logged under your name.

Your token is an admin token, so you can log on behalf of someone else. Leave it on yourself unless you are testing for a colleague.

0 = dial at once. Pause stops the countdown.

While this is filled in, every call goes to this number and the bar turns red. Outcomes and notes are still written to the real record, so use a test list.

The pipeline stage a converted lead lands in when you press Meeting booked. If empty, the first stage named like "meeting booked" is used.

Website screenshots are provided by thum.io. Without a key their free tier is used, which is slower and limited. Ask your admin for the company key.
